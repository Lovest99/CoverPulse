PRODUCT THESIS
Healthcare providers lose time and money coordinating vacant shifts through calls, spreadsheets and disconnected systems. CoverPulse helps care organisations detect coverage gaps, identify eligible workers, distribute shift offers, automate response escalation and measure coverage performance. Initial market: Australian aged-care, disability-support and community-care providers. Product wedge: specialist shift coverage layer that can integrate with existing rostering/HR systems rather than forcing full system replacement.

USER TYPES AND ACCESS
1. Platform Super Admin: manages tenant accounts, plan configuration, platform health and support access with audited, time-limited elevation.
2. Organisation Owner: manages organisation settings, facilities, billing and users.
3. Operations Manager: manages shifts, coverage policies, workers, reporting and escalations for permitted facilities.
4. Facility Manager: manages only assigned facilities.
5. Workforce Coordinator: creates shifts, monitors offers and confirms coverage.
6. Worker: maintains own profile, availability, qualifications, preferences and shift responses.
7. Auditor/Read-only: sees authorised reports and audit records without editing.
Enforce permissions server-side on every protected operation. Never rely only on hiding UI elements.

MULTI-TENANCY
- Tenant isolation must be enforced in the data access layer, not merely by UI filtering.
- Every tenant-owned record has tenant_id; facility-scoped records also have facility_id.
- Prevent cross-tenant reads, writes, exports, search results and file access.
- Add automated tenant-isolation tests.
- Support multiple facilities per organisation, configurable roles and facility-level access.
- Provide a safe demo tenant with synthetic data.

CORE MODULES
A. Executive / Manager Dashboard
- Coverage rate, open shifts, average time to first response, time to fill, estimated avoided agency/overtime cost, pending approvals and expiring credentials.
- Trends with selectable periods and facility filters.
- Upcoming shifts and urgent coverage queue.
- Every KPI must have a clear definition, date range and drill-down.
- Do not fabricate savings; label estimates and expose assumptions.

B. Shift Management
- Create, edit, duplicate, cancel and publish shifts.
- Fields: facility, date, start/end time, break, role, required skills/qualifications, headcount, urgency, notes, budget/rate visibility policy and creator.
- Support overnight shifts and timezone-safe storage; display in facility timezone.
- Validate overlapping assignments, invalid durations, missing role requirements and publication state.
- Shift states: Draft, Open, Broadcasting, Partially Covered, Covered, Cancelled, Expired.
- Keep a complete shift event timeline.

C. Workforce Directory
- Worker profile: name, contact, preferred facilities, roles, skills, qualifications, credential expiry, availability, employment/engagement type, work restrictions, preferred contact channel and status.
- Store only necessary personal information.
- Qualification evidence with access controls, expiry reminders and review status.
- Worker can update own availability and profile, subject to verification rules.
- Never infer eligibility from an AI score.

D. Eligibility and Matching Engine
- Deterministic hard filters first: active worker, correct role, required verified qualifications, facility eligibility, availability, no overlapping accepted shift, working-hour/rest constraints configured by the organisation, and any mandatory restrictions.
- Only after hard filters, rank eligible workers using transparent configurable factors: distance/travel preference where supplied, skills fit, stated preferences, response reliability and fair rotation.
- Show managers the reason a worker is included or excluded; never expose sensitive attributes or protected-class inference.
- AI may summarise options and explain trade-offs but cannot override hard eligibility rules.
- Provide manual override only for authorised users, with mandatory reason and audit event; never permit bypass of legal or safety-critical requirements.
- Build unit tests for edge cases and fairness review hooks.

E. Coverage Autopilot
- Manager selects a shift and a coverage policy.
- Offer dispatch can be sequential, tiered or controlled parallel waves.
- Configure response windows, candidate batch sizes, quiet hours, maximum attempts, reminders and escalation recipients.
- Channels: in-app, email and SMS through provider adapters.
- Track delivery, bounce, failure, opened (only where available), accepted, declined, expired and withdrawn events.
- Use idempotency keys, retries with backoff, provider rate limits and dead-letter handling.
- Stop further offers when the shift is fully covered or cancelled.
- Escalate to coordinator/manager when no eligible worker accepts.
- Never silently mark a shift covered; require an accepted eligible worker and any configured manager confirmation.
- Prevent double booking under concurrent acceptance using database transactions/locking.

F. Worker Experience (mobile-first PWA)
- Sign in securely.
- Home screen: next shift, open offers, alerts and availability status.
- Offer detail: facility, role, date/time, expected duties, required credentials, pay details only where authorised, response deadline and contact instructions.
- Accept/decline with confirmation and clear outcome.
- View upcoming/past shifts, availability calendar, profile and qualifications.
- Push notification capability where supported; graceful email/SMS fallback.
- Accessible controls, low-bandwidth performance and clear offline/read-only behaviour.
- Never allow offline acceptance to be treated as final until server confirmation.

G. Analytics and Reporting
- Fill rate = covered eligible shift positions / total published shift positions for selected period.
- Time to first response and time to fill; define start/end events precisely.
- Unfilled hours, late cancellations, offer acceptance rates, response by channel, facility and role.
- Estimated cost impact must be configurable and transparently calculated from customer-provided assumptions.
- Export CSV and PDF with permission checks and audit logging.
- No employee performance ranking that could be misleading or used without context.

H. Integrations
- Integration settings and connection status.
- Design adapter interfaces for rostering, HRIS, payroll and identity systems.
- Start with CSV import/export and documented API/webhook contracts.
- Webhook signing, replay protection, idempotency and delivery logs.
- Do not claim integrations exist until implemented and tested.
- Provide integration sandbox and sample payloads.

I. Billing and Plans
- Essential, Growth and Enterprise plan configuration.
- Subscription status, trial, invoices, payment method link, usage and plan limits.
- Pricing must be configurable by platform admin; do not hard-code launch prices into business logic.
- Verify billing webhooks and handle retries and duplicate events.
- Clearly distinguish subscription fees from usage-based SMS or integration charges.

J. Administration, Audit and Security
- Organisation profile, facilities, roles, policies, notification templates and working-hour rules.
- Immutable audit trail for sign-in/security events, worker changes, credential review, shift edits, offer dispatch, acceptance, override, export and admin actions.
- MFA-ready authentication, secure password reset, session revocation, rate limiting, CSRF protections where relevant, secure headers, input validation and least privilege.
- Encrypt data in transit and at rest through managed infrastructure.
- Backups, restore procedures, retention policies and deletion workflows.
- Privacy request workflow and data export.
- Australian Privacy Act / APP considerations, applicable state/territory requirements, NDIS and aged-care obligations must be reviewed by qualified counsel and compliance specialists. Do not claim compliance solely because controls exist.
- Provide a security and privacy implementation checklist.

ACCEPTANCE SCENARIO
A manager at Facility A creates an urgent RN night shift requiring verified qualifications. The engine filters out unavailable, unqualified, restricted and overlapping workers, then ranks eligible candidates transparently. The manager starts an automated coverage policy. Workers receive offers, one accepts, the server atomically confirms the assignment, further offers stop, the manager sees the covered status and event timeline, and analytics update. A second scenario must demonstrate no acceptance, escalation to a manager and an auditable unresolved shift. A third must prove that a worker from another tenant cannot access any of the organisation's records.
